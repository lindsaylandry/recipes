# Pizza Dough

## Ingredients

* 3/4 cup warm water
* 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
* 1 package (1/4 ounce) active dry yeast
* 3 1/2 cups flour
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
OR
* 3 tablespoons pork fat

## Directions

1. Mix warm water, sugar, and yeast in a heat-safe cup (glass jar). Let sit for 5 minutes.
1. Mix other dry ingredients in a large bowl.
1. If using oil, add this in.
1. If using lard, add this in, breaking it up into 5-6 smaller pieces.
1. If using an electric mixer such as a KitchenAid, start on a low setting until the dough comes together; it might seem a little dry at first but as it kneads, the dough will actually be quite wet and sticky.
1. Once dough comes together, turn the mixer to a medium setting and knead for about 8 minutes. 
1. If you don’t have a KitchenAid:
  1. Start in a large mixing bowl
  1. use a spoon to stir the ingredients until they start to come together
  1. Transfer to a large clean surface (such as a large wooden cutting board) and proceed to knead by hand until the dough is smooth and elastic. 
1. Transfer dough to a lightly greased bowl (of course using lard for this part). Cover tightly with plastic film and set aside.
1. Let sit for 2 hours until dough has doubled in size
1. remove from bowl on to a clean and lightly floured surface.
1. Divide dough into 2 equal balls, cover with a damp kitchen towel and let to rest and rise again for another 2-3 hours, or until it has doubled in size again.
1. Roll dough out onto pan and add toppings.
1. Bake at 400 degrees for 15-20 minutes.
