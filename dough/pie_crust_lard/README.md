# Pie Crust (Lard)

## Ingredients
1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
½ tsp salt
½ cup cold pork fat
3 to 4 tbsp ice-cold water

## Instructions

1. Mix Dry Ingredients: Whisk the flour and salt together in a large bowl.
1. Cut in the Fat: Add the cold lard. Use a pastry blender, two forks, or your fingers to cut and rub the fat into the flour until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs with pea-sized pieces of fat.
1. Add Water: Sprinkle the ice water over the mixture one tablespoon at a time. Stir gently with a fork until the dough just begins to clump together. Do not overmix.
1. Shape and Chill: Turn the dough onto a clean surface. Shape it into a flat disc, wrap it tightly in plastic wrap, and place it in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
1. Roll Out: Roll the chilled dough out on a lightly floured surface to your desired size, then transfer it to your pie plate.
